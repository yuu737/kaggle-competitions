Kaggriculture Foundation v0, 2026-09-30

The agent policy, investment search, worker scheduler, and experiment tools are
new implementations. No public competitor agent or past route library is used
inside the runtime agent. Past agents are read only by the evaluation harness
as explicitly identified opponents.

Game constants and the behavior implemented in farm_model/rules.py,
farm_model/market.py, and farm_model/physics.py derive from Kaggle's official
kaggriculture environment in kaggle-environments 1.32.7.
